# Project scope

## Stakeholder
The Head of Complaints & Customer Operations at Chime Financial Inc

## Question
"Where do our customers complain most, where do we respond poorly, and what should we fix first?"

## Data
- Source: CFPB Consumer Complaint Database (public), downloaded 14 Oct 2026.
- Window: complaints received 1 Jan 2024 to 31 Dec 2025.
- Products: Credit Card; Checking or Savings Account; Money Transfer, Virtual Currency, or Money Service.
- Size: 404,353 complaints, all companies kept as the market benchmark.
- US data.

## In scope
1. Volume and trend by product and month
2. Root causes: issues and sub-issues per product
3. Service performance: % timely response and % closed with relief (the data has no company response date, so response speed in days cannot be measured)
4. Company comparison against peers (rates, not raw counts); credit bureaus are treated separately from banks because they make up 26% of the credit card complaints
5. Top 3 findings and recommendations

## Out of scope
- Narrative text analysis (not in the data)
- Prediction / machine learning
- Complaint rates per customer (no customer counts in the data)
- Other products (mortgages, loans, credit reporting)
- Proving causes

## Known limits
Self-reported, not a random sample; more complaints can mean not worse service but rather more awareness or filing.
